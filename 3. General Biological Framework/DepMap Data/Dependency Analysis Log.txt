+ Interpretation:

Expression Columns (_Expr): These are the Log2(TPM+1) values from DepMap. They tell you the base abundance of the mRNA. You successfully retrieved the data for your normal proxies (BJ_Expr and WI-38_Expr)!

Dependency Columns (_RNAi): These are the DEMETER2 scores.How to analyze: Look for numbers <= -0.5. In the snippet, ACLY has an RNAi score of -0.689 in HL-60 and -0.830 in MCF-7, meaning knocking it down kills both cell types. However, ADK is -0.66 in HL-60 but +0.006 in MCF-7, meaning HL-60 is critically addicted to ADK, while MCF-7 doesn't care if it loses it.

Log2FC_HL60_vs_BJ: This is the Therapeutic Window. How to analyze: Positive numbers are better, ideally > 1.0. This means HL-60 expresses more of the target than healthy skin cells (BJ).

Delta_RNAi_HL60_vs_MCF7: This is the Selective Lethality. How to analyze: You want highly negative numbers. A negative number means the knockdown was much more toxic to HL-60 than to MCF-7. For example, ADK has a $\Delta$ of -0.666.
